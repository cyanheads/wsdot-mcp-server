# 0.6.0 — 2026-11-12

## Added

- **`wsdot_get_ferry_fares`** — WSF fares for a terminal pair on a date, one way or round trip, with WSF's statement of which terminal collects which fares leading the output ([#58](https://github.com/cyanheads/wsdot-mcp-server/issues/58)).
- **`…Local` Pacific siblings on every ferry time** — `departureTime`, `arrivalTime`, `scheduledDeparture`, `leftDock`, `eta`, `timestamp`, `departure`, and `publishDate` each gain a `<field>Local` value with its UTC offset; `content[]` renders local, then UTC ([#59](https://github.com/cyanheads/wsdot-mcp-server/issues/59)).
- **`serviceDate`** on every `wsdot_get_ferry_schedule` sailing, the Pacific service day whose schedule lists it ([#55](https://github.com/cyanheads/wsdot-mcp-server/issues/55)).
- **`owner` and `ownerUrl`** on `wsdot_search_cameras` results and in `content[]`; an image belongs to its listed owner, or to WSDOT when none is listed ([#62](https://github.com/cyanheads/wsdot-mcp-server/issues/62)).
- **`titleMilePost`** on cameras, the milepost written in the title; a milepost search matches either value and never returns fewer cameras than 0.5.0 ([#48](https://github.com/cyanheads/wsdot-mcp-server/issues/48)).
- **`WSDOT_CLAMP_LIMITS`** (default `true`) — switches the `limit` clamp below; each clamp writes one log line and increments the `wsdot.limit.clamps` counter.

## Changed

- **A `limit` above a paged tool's maximum is clamped** (500; 20 for `wsdot_get_terminal_space`) with a notice instead of rejected, and `limit` advertises no `maximum` while clamping; `WSDOT_CLAMP_LIMITS=false` restores rejection and the `maximum` ([#63](https://github.com/cyanheads/wsdot-mcp-server/issues/63)).
- **`wsdot-guide` skill** prices trips with the fares tool, quotes Pacific times, and covers the post-midnight service day and terminals without space data.
- Sync framework skills, scripts, and the changelog template to mcp-ts-core 0.13.22; join three tool descriptions into single-line literals (text unchanged).

## Fixed

- **`wsdot_get_ferry_schedule`** — before 03:00 Pacific, `remainingOnly` lists the previous service day's sailings still to depart first; a failed previous-day read returns today's list with a notice ([#55](https://github.com/cyanheads/wsdot-mcp-server/issues/55)).
- **Page size** — `wsdot_search_alerts`, `wsdot_search_cameras`, `wsdot_get_travel_times`, and `wsdot_get_toll_rates` end a page only at `limit` or the end of the set; the 24,000-byte page budget is gone ([#57](https://github.com/cyanheads/wsdot-mcp-server/issues/57)).
- **`wsdot_get_travel_times`** — a route designation matches a corridor's endpoints or a designation written in its name, never digits inside another number: `5`, `SR 5`, and other forms whose digits sat inside another route no longer return its corridors (I-405, SR 522), and `I-90` and `SR 520` now include the "via 90" and "via 520" corridors ([#56](https://github.com/cyanheads/wsdot-mcp-server/issues/56)).
- **`wsdot_get_terminal_space`** — an empty feed and a terminal the feed does not cover get distinct notices; the second lists the covered terminals ([#60](https://github.com/cyanheads/wsdot-mcp-server/issues/60)).
- **`wsdot_search_alerts`** — headlines and extended descriptions render line by line in `content[]`, trimmed, with no `&#32;` or lone `\` lines ([#61](https://github.com/cyanheads/wsdot-mcp-server/issues/61)).
- **Ferry call deadlines** — `wsdot_get_ferry_schedule`, `wsdot_get_ferry_routes`, and `wsdot_get_ferry_fares` settle within 50 seconds and fail as `retry_deadline_exceeded` (`Timeout`) with a retry hint; an exhausted retry's deadline message and `data.operation` now read "WSF Ferry API request" or "WSDOT Traffic API request", not an internal method name.
